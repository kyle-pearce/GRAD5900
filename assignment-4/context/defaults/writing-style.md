# Writing Style

## Core Characteristics

### Structure & Organization
- **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front)**: Always leads with executive summary or key takeaway
- **Clear Sectioning**: Uses headers to organize content logically
- **Structured Document Conventions**: Follows standard formats (status/owners/date headers, 1-pagers, appendices)
- **Progressive Detail**: Starts high-level, then provides supporting detail for those who need it

### Analytical Rigor
- **Quantitative Thinking**: Heavy use of numbers, percentages, calculations
- **Shows Work**: Doesn't just state conclusions — shows formulas, assumptions, methodology
- **Statistical Literacy**: Comfortable with confidence levels, margin of error, sample sizes
- **Data-Driven**: Backs up claims with empirical data (response rates, hours saved, population sizes)

### Audience Awareness
- **Anticipates Questions**: Proactively addresses "what about X?" before the reader asks
- **Risk Transparency**: Explicitly calls out assumptions and risks
- **Stakeholder Mapping**: Clear about who needs to do what by when
- **Appropriate Detail Level**: Technical depth for technical docs, executive summary for leadership

### Strategic Framing
- **Comparison Structure**: "Before vs. After" or "Last Year vs. This Year" to show progress
- **Impact Quantification**: Translate work into concrete outcomes (hours saved, efficiency gained)
- **Future Vision**: Always includes a "Future Plans" or "Next Steps" section
- **Problem → Solution**: Clearly articulates the problem before proposing a solution

### Tone & Voice
- **Professional but Direct**: No fluff, gets to the point
- **Confident**: States recommendations clearly ("I recommend...")
- **Collaborative**: Uses "we" frequently, acknowledges team contributions
- **Pragmatic**: Focuses on what's achievable, not just ideal

## Writing Habits to Reinforce
- BLUF approach (don't bury the lede)
- Quantify everything possible
- Show your work (builds credibility)
- Always include next steps
- Use consistent, structured document formats

## Writing Habits to Change
- May need to emphasize strategic leadership framing more explicitly in written artifacts
